What We Do

Rotating equipment is the backbone of Texas industry — pumps, compressors and turbomachinery keep refineries, plants and pipelines running, and the hiring landscape around them is tightening fast.

Field service technicians and reliability engineers with real equipment experience are scarce, and the best of them are rarely on a job board — they're already working, already known to a handful of OEMs and service organisations, and rarely reachable through a generic vacancy ad.

For businesses hiring rotating equipment talent, that scarcity brings a very real recruitment challenge: if you wait for the right person to apply, a competitor has probably already reached them first.

That's where Darwin comes in.

Our specialist consultants combine equipment knowledge with Texas market intelligence — built specifically for OEMs, industrial solution providers and end users across the Gulf Coast, Permian and DFW.

Whether you're hiring field service technicians, reliability and commissioning engineers, or the commercial leaders who sell and grow the business, we know where to find people who never show up on job boards.
